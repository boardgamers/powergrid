## What is bound

The inventory reads launch/terminal metadata and hashes existing review files
without parsing their outcome contents. Complete-study admission requires all
324 recovery assignments before invoking any review loader. Each subsequent
authentication binds the canonical row, exact HF handle, original failure link,
prepared source and admission, original bootstrap/environment hashes, effective
24h CPU allocation, collector/manager versions, terminal log, unique immutable
final archive receipt, envelope control files and original raw manifest.

The reviewed games must cover the exact episode interval, use the frozen policy
and shard, and have uncapped verified receipts. No failed original can be
relabelled successful. The independent failed-shard recovery must retain the
specific original ERROR handle and its separate admitted handle. Remaining rows
must retain their first-execution status and durable launch intent. None of these
checks can waive the full raw re-audit required before statistics.
