# Power Grid — Chile

_The map of Chile can only be played with a copy of Power Grid or Power Grid (Recharged Version)._

The rules of Power Grid remain the same, except for the following modifications and special features of the Chile map.

---

## Introduction

For most of its history, Chile ran on two separate grids. The Norte Grande grid ran from Arica to Antofagasta and powered the copper mines of the Atacama Desert. The Central grid powered the rest of the country, reaching north into the Atacama Region as far as Taltal. In the mid-2000s, Argentina cut off the gas that both grids depended on. The two grids were finally joined in 2017, as solar power took off in the desert.

In this version of the game, the players start out on two separate grids, each with its own resource market. Chile has no nuclear power. In Step 3 the grids are joined: the markets merge, the connections between the grids open, and the great solar plants of the Atacama come online.

---

## The Map

Every city on the map belongs to one of the two grids: the **North grid** or the **Central grid**. The grid of a city is shown on the map. The two northern regions reach south across the divide, so each of them has cities in both grids.

| Region                      | North grid                                                 | Central grid                                                                                  |
| --------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Northern coast (brown)      | Arica, Iquique, Tocopilla, Mejillones, Antofagasta         | Taltal, Chañaral                                                                              |
| Atacama Desert (yellow)     | Pozo Almonte, María Elena, Calama, San Pedro, Sierra Gorda | El Salvador, Copiapó                                                                          |
| Norte Chico (purple)        | —                                                          | Caldera, Huasco, Vallenar, La Serena, Ovalle, Los Vilos, Illapel                              |
| Santiago & Valparaíso (red) | —                                                          | Los Andes, Viña del Mar, Quillota, Santiago N, Valparaíso, Santiago W, San Antonio, Melipilla |
| Central Valley (green)      | —                                                          | Santiago E, Santiago S, Rancagua, Curicó, San Fernando, Talca, Chillán                        |
| Biobío & the South (pink)   | —                                                          | Santa Cruz, Constitución, Cauquenes, Concepción, Los Ángeles, Temuco, Valdivia, Puerto Montt  |

**Gates:** the grids are joined only by two gated connections, one in each northern region:
→ **Antofagasta – Taltal** (Northern coast), cost 13.
→ **Sierra Gorda – El Salvador** (Atacama Desert), cost 20.

**Greater Santiago:** Santiago N, Santiago E, Santiago S and Santiago W are joined in a ring of connections that cost 0. The ring is split between the red and green regions.

**Border spaces:** three spaces lie across the borders of the North, outside every region. They belong to the North grid.
→ **Salta** (Argentina), connected to San Pedro: always in play.
→ **Jujuy** (Argentina), connected to San Pedro: with 4–6 players only.
→ **Tacna** (Peru), connected to Arica: with 4–6 players only.

## Preparation

**Play areas:** the Northern coast (brown) and the Atacama Desert (yellow) are always in play. Choose the remaining regions as usual, each connected to the regions already in play:
→ **with 2 and 3 players:** brown, yellow, purple.
→ **with 4 players:** brown, yellow, purple, red.
→ **with 5 and 6 players:** brown, yellow, purple, red, and green or pink.

With 2 and 3 players, Tacna and Jujuy are not in play.

**Power plants:** Chile has no nuclear power. Remove all uranium power plants from the game and place them in the box. Then set aside the three largest ecological power plants; they enter the game in Step 3.

| Power plant deck         | Remove (uranium)       | Set aside (ecological) |
| ------------------------ | ---------------------- | ---------------------- |
| Standard                 | 11, 17, 23, 28, 34, 39 | 33, 37, 44             |
| New Power Plants, deck 1 | 8, 19, 24, 31, 38, 50  | 32, 39, 44             |
| New Power Plants, deck 2 | 13, 21, 32, 37, 50     | 31, 36, 44             |

> **Important:** do not remove any further power plants for the number of players.

**Resource markets:** Chile has two separate resource markets, one for the North grid and one for the Central grid. Fill the following spaces:
→ **North:** coal - all spaces, oil - all spaces. The North market has no garbage.
→ **Central:** coal - all spaces, oil - spaces 4–8, garbage - spaces 7–8.

The remaining resources form one supply, shared by both markets. No uranium is used.

**Gates:** place a Step 3 marker on each of the two gated connections.

---

## Phase 3: Buying Resources

Until Step 3, each player may only buy resources from the market of the grid their cities are in. Because only the gates join the two grids, all of a player's cities are in the same grid until Step 3.

> **Important:** a player with no cities (as in the first round) chooses one of the two markets and makes all of their purchases for the round from that market.

If the market a player buys from has no more of a resource they want, they cannot buy that resource this round. North players cannot buy garbage until Step 3.

---

## Phase 4: Building

**Gates:** no player may build across a gated connection until Step 3.

**Border spaces:** each border space holds only one city. Building there costs the connection plus 10 Elektro, the same as a first house in any city.

> **Important:** no player may build their first city in a border space.

---

## Phase 5: Bureaucracy

Restock the **North market first**, then the **Central market**, from the shared supply, using the resource refill table below. If the supply runs short, the North market is filled first as far as possible.

> **Important:** garbage is only restocked in the Central market. In Step 2, oil is restocked 1 per market regardless of the number of players.

---

## Step 3

When Step 3 begins, carry it out as usual. Then:

1. **Merge the markets.** Take all resources from both markets and place them on the standard resource market, filling it from the most expensive spaces. From now on there is only one market, restocked using the Step 3 column of the refill table.
2. **Open the gates.** Remove the Step 3 markers. Players may now build across the gated connections, and may buy from the merged market wherever their cities are.
3. **Add Atacama solar.** Place the three set-aside ecological power plants on top of the power plant supply.
4. **Boost ecological plants.** For the rest of the game, every ecological power plant powers one more city than printed.

> **Important:** the fusion power plant does not get the extra city.

---

## Resource Refill

In Steps 1 and 2, the first number restocks the North market and the second number the Central market. Garbage always goes to the Central market. In Step 3, there is only one market.

| Players | Coal (Step 1 / 2 / 3) | Oil (Step 1 / 2 / 3) | Garbage (Step 1 / 2 / 3) |
| ------- | --------------------- | -------------------- | ------------------------ |
| 2       | 2+2 / 3+3 / 4         | 1+1 / 1+1 / 4        | 1 / 2 / 4                |
| 3       | 1+3 / 3+3 / 4         | 1+1 / 1+1 / 4        | 1 / 2 / 4                |
| 4       | 2+3 / 3+4 / 5         | 1+2 / 1+1 / 5        | 2 / 3 / 5                |
| 5       | 2+3 / 3+5 / 4         | 2+2 / 1+1 / 5        | 3 / 3 / 4                |
| 6       | 3+4 / 4+6 / 6         | 2+3 / 1+1 / 7        | 3 / 5 / 6                |

_Example: with 3 players in Step 1, restock 1 coal and 1 oil in the North market, then 3 coal, 1 oil and 1 garbage in the Central market._
